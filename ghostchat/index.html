No. It is built for Web3 browsing in general, with context tied to each active site session.

Can I control notifications and visibility?

Yes. Notifications are optional, and you can tune settings like theme, profile, and danmaku overlay behavior.

Is GhostChat a security guarantee?

No. Safety signals are support tools that provide fast heuristics. Always verify independently before sensitive actions.

What makes GhostChat different?

It combines social context, momentum tracking, and risk cues directly inside the browsing surface where decisions happen.
