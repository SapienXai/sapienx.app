Paddle Toolkit

Build, test, debug, and operate Paddle Billing integrations with focused workflows for ChatGPT and Codex.

Publisher: Kazım Akgül

Paddle Toolkit is independently published and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Paddle.

What it helps with

  • Plan Paddle Billing architecture for an existing application.
  • Design products, prices, and localized pricing pages.
  • Implement checkout, webhooks, subscription sync, and entitlements.
  • Test against Paddle sandbox and review production readiness.
  • Connect directly to Paddle's official MCP through OAuth for explicitly requested live account actions.

How it works

Paddle Toolkit is a skills-only instruction package. It does not run a billing backend, proxy Paddle API requests, or store Paddle credentials. Account operations connect directly from your client to Paddle's service. Sandbox and live access remain separate.
